# Commercial Licensing

VelvetMarkets Trading SDK is proprietary commercial software.

Standard licensing is designed for use within an authorised application.

Commercial plans may provide access to capabilities including:

- research intelligence
- trading signals
- risk infrastructure
- audit logging
- broker adapters
- live-execution integration

Multi-application, resale, white-label, private deployment and bespoke broker requirements are handled through Enterprise & Bespoke licensing.

The public showcase repository does not contain licence-signing secrets, private keys, broker credentials, customer credentials or the proprietary SDK implementation.
