## Licensing

Trading SDK and Trading SDK Pro licences cover one application for one annual licence period.

The SDK remains the intellectual property of Velvet Technologies.

Purchase of a licence does not transfer ownership of the SDK source code or permit redistribution, resale or sublicensing unless explicitly agreed under an Enterprise licence.

VelvetMarkets Trading SDK provides software infrastructure and trading intelligence. It is not a broker, investment adviser or managed trading service.
